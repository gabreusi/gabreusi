## About

Full stack engineer, strongest on the front end. Over two years I worked on **ConecteMe**, a retail integration platform that connects client operations to marketplaces and ERPs. Most of it was the internal front-end framework its seven modules run on: rendering performance, state handling, and real types for a codebase that was almost entirely `any`.

I also built the platform's omnichannel support chat (WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger in one thread), design included.
